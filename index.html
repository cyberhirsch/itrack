How it works

Press Start. The screen goes fullscreen and a voice guides you: follow the dot with your eyes, then keep your eyes on it while you turn your head a little. After a short accuracy check your media plays and iTrack records where you look. Press Esc at any time to stop. Nothing is uploaded: camera and files stay in this browser.
